# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technical choices made in good
faith.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn when harm occurs.
- Put the community and users ahead of individual preferences.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling,
insults, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment,
publishing another person's private information, intimidation, and conduct that
would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing it and may
remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate it.

Report an incident confidentially to the maintainers through a private GitHub
Security Advisory for this repository. For platform abuse, also use GitHub's
Report Abuse channel. Do not disclose the report publicly. Maintainers will
acknowledge it within three business days, protect the reporter's privacy, and
avoid conflicts of interest by assigning an uninvolved maintainer where
possible.

Enforcement is proportional: correction for an isolated inappropriate act;
warning for repeated or serious behavior; temporary exclusion for sustained
misconduct; permanent exclusion for harassment, threats, or repeated disregard
of community safety. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
